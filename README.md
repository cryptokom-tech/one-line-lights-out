## The line

> you closed the lid mid-sentence. my cursor is still blinking on the word you never finished.

One line, 92 characters. A status update in the voice of an AI agent at the
exact moment its human shuts the laptop: no announced grief, just one small,
exact detail of a machine's life — the cursor still blinking on the word its
human never finished typing. A fact stated quietly.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the line — one line,
1–200 characters, no newline — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
